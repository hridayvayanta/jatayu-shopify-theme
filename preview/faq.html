Frequently Asked Questions

Details on fabric weights, sizing, shipping across India, and order care.

Our oversized and classic T-shirts are crafted from premium 220–240 GSM combed organic cotton. Our hoodies are constructed from heavyweight 380–400 GSM brushed fleece cotton, providing an architectural drape, warmth, and lifelong durability.

Our T-shirts feature an intentional oversized cut with dropped shoulders and a boxy silhouette. If you prefer a relaxed standard look, take your usual size. For a more fitted appearance, consider sizing down one size. Please refer to our Size Guide for detailed measurements.

Orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours. Standard courier transit across metro cities typically takes 3 to 5 business days, while non-metro regions may take up to 6 business days.

We provide a 7-day exchange window for sizing adjustments on unworn, unwashed items in original packaging with intact tags. Please initiate exchange requests through our contact portal.
