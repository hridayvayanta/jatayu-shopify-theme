The Five Pillars of Every Garment

  1. 01

    Raw Material & 240 GSM Weight Check

    We source long-staple Indian organic cotton combed twice to extract microscopic short fibers. Weighed on calibrated analytical scales to ensure a dense, luxurious 240 GSM drape that hangs with architectural presence.

  2. 02

    Screen Ink & Cured Foil Durability Test

    Our sacred glyphs and Devanagari script undergo high-temperature heat-curing at 180°C. Stretched in 4 directions and tested through 50+ wash cycles to verify zero cracking, peeling, or fading.

  3. 03

    Architectural Drop-Shoulder Drape Check

    Every garment pattern is hand-checked on custom mannequins. The dropped shoulder seam is engineered at an exact 14-degree slope so the silhouette stays relaxed yet structured all day.

  4. 04

    Double-Needle Reinforced Structural Seams

    Collars, armholes, and hems are secured with high-tensile twin-needle lockstitching and 1.25" heavyweight 1x1 cotton ribbing, preventing the collar from ever curling or sagging over time.

  5. 05

    Archival Steam Press & Sealed Collector Pouch

    Each piece is individually inspected under high-CRI inspection lights, hand-steamed, and hermetically sealed in biodegradable frosted pouches with brass-embossed Jatayu collector certificates.

CLARITY & STANDARDS

Frequently Answered Enquiries

What makes JATAYU 240 GSM cotton distinct from regular T-shirts? +

Standard retail t-shirts range between 140–180 GSM and feel flimsy. JATAYU garments are woven at a hefty 240 GSM using double-combed organic cotton. This creates a substantial, structured drape that doesn't cling to the body, maintains its silhouette throughout the day, and softens gracefully with every wear.

How does the oversized silhouette fit? Should I size down? +

Our Sovereign Oversized Tees are engineered with a relaxed, dropped shoulder and elongated sleeve length. If you desire the signature streetwear oversized drape, choose your true size. If you prefer a more tailored, traditional classic fit, choose one size down. Refer to our Size Guide for exact chest measurements.

How should I wash and care for my JATAYU garments? +

Machine wash cold (30°C or below) inside-out with similar dark or earth tones. Use mild detergent. Do not bleach. Line dry in shade to preserve color intensity and print vibrancy. Iron on reverse at medium heat; never touch the screen-printed or embroidered areas directly with a hot iron.

What are the dispatch timelines and return policies? +

All orders are dispatched from our Ayodhya & regional fulfillment centers within 24 to 48 hours via Bluedart and express air couriers (delivering in 2–5 business days pan-India). We offer a 7-day hassle-free size exchange policy on unworn garments with original archival tags intact.
